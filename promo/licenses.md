## Sound

Every sound is a recording from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com), used under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free for commercial use, no attribution required. The files themselves may not be redistributed as standalone audio, so they are not committed; `sound/mix.py` fetches any missing file from Pixabay's CDN. Nothing is synthesized: the mix only trims, fades, levels, filters and mixes these recordings.
